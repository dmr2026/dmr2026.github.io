## Organizing Committee

**[Jan Hajič](mailto:hajic@ufal.mff.cuni.cz)** — Charles University, Czech Republic
: Professor of Computational Linguistics at the Institute of Formal and Applied Linguistics and a leader of
the LINDAT/CLARIAH-CZ research infrastructure. His work includes richly annotated treebanks, deep
syntactic and semantic representation, multilingual language resources, machine translation, and empirical NLP.

**[Adriana Pagano](mailto:apagano@ufmg.br)** — Federal University of Minas Gerais (UFMG), Brazil
: Full Professor at UFMG, working on multilingual text production, translation, linguistic modeling,
human–computer interaction, and semantic representation. Her recent work brings Uniform Meaning
Representation to the Portuguese-language NLP community, including a UMR tutorial at PROPOR 2026.

**[James Pustejovsky](mailto:jamesp@brandeis.edu)** — Brandeis University, USA
: TJX Feldberg Professor of Computer Science and Chair of Linguistics and Computational Linguistics at
Brandeis. His research spans lexical and computational semantics, corpus annotation, temporal and spatial
reasoning, and artificial intelligence, including TimeML and UMR.

**[Shira Wein](mailto:shirawein@usf.edu)** — University of South Florida, USA
: Assistant Professor in the Bellini College of Artificial Intelligence, Cybersecurity and Computing. Her
research centers on multilingual language modeling, computational semantics, and model assessment, with
substantial work on AMR/UMR parsing, generation, cross-lingual transfer, and the downstream utility of
linguistic representations.

**[Nianwen Xue](mailto:xuen@brandeis.edu)** — Brandeis University, USA
: Professor of Computer Science and Linguistics and Chair of Computer Science at Brandeis. His research
focuses on syntactic, semantic, and discourse representation and parsing, multilingual NLP, event
understanding, and computational social science. He is a principal investigator of the Uniform Meaning
Representation project.

**[Jin Zhao](mailto:jin.zhao@slu.edu)** — Saint Louis University, USA
: Assistant Professor of Computer Science at Saint Louis University. Her research connects event
understanding, computational framing, multilingual semantics, and human-centered AI, with an emphasis on how
different sources describe the same real-world events.

The organizing team spans North America, Europe, and South America and includes researchers at different
career stages with expertise in English, Chinese, Czech, Portuguese, and multilingual semantics.

## Program Committee

The program committee is being recruited and will be listed here once membership is confirmed. We aim for a
committee large enough to guarantee three reviews per submission while assigning no reviewer more than three
papers, with attention to gender, geography, career stage, institution type, and linguistic community.

If you would like to serve as a reviewer for DMR 2027, please write to
[dmrworkshopofficial@gmail.com](mailto:dmrworkshopofficial@gmail.com).
