## Venue

<div class="notice" markdown="1">
The host conference and location for DMR 2027 have not been confirmed yet. They will be posted here as soon
as the workshop proposal is accepted and the venue is assigned.
</div>

DMR 2027 is planned as a **one-day workshop** with hybrid participation: all oral sessions will support
remote presentation and questions through the host conference platform, and remote attendees will be able to
contribute questions to the panel discussion on equal terms.

Previous editions were co-located with ACL, COLING, EMNLP, IWCS, LREC, LREC-COLING, and SyntaxFest — see
[past editions]({{ '/editions/' | relative_url }}).
